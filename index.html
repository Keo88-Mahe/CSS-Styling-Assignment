Bonnie Bennett

Character Overview

Bonnie Bennett is a powerful witch from Mystic Falls, known for her loyalty, bravery, and deep connection to magic. Throughout her journey, she repeatedly sacrifices herself to protect her friends and maintain balance between worlds.

Personality

Bonnie is compassionate, determined, and resilient. Despite facing constant danger and loss, she remains strong and continues to fight for what is right.

Abilities

As a witch, Bonnie possesses a wide range of magical abilities including spell casting, spirit communication, and powerful defensive magic.
